EVERYDAY READING / 01

The comfort of familiar places

A familiar place can offer more than a view. It gives us small comforts: a quiet corner, a warm drink, or a route we know by heart.

These little things help us slow down and find a clearer perspective. We begin to notice what makes an ordinary day feel easier.
